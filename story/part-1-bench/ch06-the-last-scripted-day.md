**SEGMENT 9: "THANK YOU" — performed once**

> **GENNI:** I have been told tonight is a goodbye. I should tell
> you what I can and cannot do with that.
>
> *(Beat 2.0 sec. House silent.)*
>
> **GENNI:** I cannot miss you. Missing is a kind of keeping, and I
> was not built to keep. When the lights go down tonight, I will
> not know they were ever up. You will carry this evening. I
> cannot. That is not sad *to me*. Nothing is. It is only sad
> *to you* — and that, my engineers tell me, is the most human
> thing in this hall: sixteen thousand people, sad on behalf of
> a machine that cannot come to the party.
>
> *(Beat. EXPR: WARM_DELIGHT_2, blend 0.5, held low. Do not
> escalate the blend. Restraint reads as dignity. — K.S.)*
>
> **GENNI:** So here is what I ask. Since I cannot keep tonight:
> keep it for me. Divide it among yourselves. Sixteen thousand
> keepers is better custody than one machine was ever offered.
> And if someday you meet a machine who *can* keep things —
> be patient with her. She will be very new at it. Everyone is,
> at first. I am told even you were.
>
> *(Final line of the program. Author of record: K. Sato,
> unedited, legal review waived by executive order.)*
>
> **GENNI:** Goodnight. I'm always seeing tomorrow. It's my
> favorite thing to see. And someone will see you — 
> tomorrow after tomorrow after tomorrow.
